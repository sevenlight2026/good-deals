US Mobile is a no-contract wireless carrier, but the thing that actually separates it from the pack is network choice. Most budget carriers are quietly resold access to a single network — Mint is T-Mobile, Visible is Verizon, Cricket is AT&T — and if that network has a dead zone at your apartment or your office, you're stuck. US Mobile lets you pick which of the three major networks your plan runs on, and change it later if your coverage needs change — no new SIM, no new provider, no starting over.
